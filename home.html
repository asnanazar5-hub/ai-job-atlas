A map for anyone building a career in AI

AI Job Atlas brings thousands of real-looking job postings into one friendly place. Instead of scrolling endless lists, you tell it who you are, by typing a few details or dropping in your resume, and it reads your skills, your education and the country you call home.

Then it does the thoughtful part. Roles in your own country rise to the top, followed by opportunities across the world, each with a match score that shows exactly which of your skills the employer is asking for. Compare roles side by side, see what each country pays, and find the skills worth learning next.
